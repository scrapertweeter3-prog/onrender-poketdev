Cancel tomorrow and keep everything shipped today

The unusual promise at Poket Dev is not the flat rate, it is the exit. Every deliverable lands in a private GitHub repository under your own account, so if the subscription ends, the work stays. Nothing gets held hostage on a vendor branch or in an agency-held org. That single term changes how the rest of the service behaves: work ships in small approved slices, you inspect each one in the repo, and the relationship only continues while the deliveries keep convincing you.

The subscription itself

One card moves at a time on a Trello board you can see. Requests are unlimited and stack up as cards; each one gets built within roughly 72 hours Monday through Friday, then moves to approved only when you sign off. Revisions ride along as comments on the card and run as long as the card needs. There are no meetings anywhere in the loop; the board is the conversation. Onboarding takes about a day: your board goes live, you file the first card, the founder starts building.

Prices that read like a utility bill

Three numbers exist: $5,995 month to month, $5,495 on quarterly billing, $4,995 a month billed yearly. Nothing is due upfront and the plan runs until you end it. The site prints its own comparison to hiring, where a fully loaded senior US engineer runs about $18,290 a month once benefits, taxes, bonus, and retirement enter the math. Whether that gap holds for your roadmap is checkable line by line on poketdev.com.
